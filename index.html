Who We Are

A Section 8 NGO
built on compassion

Founded by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Central Government-licensed non-profit. We hold 80G & 12A certifications for donor transparency, are CSR-1 registered for corporate partnerships, NITI Aayog registered, and carry the IAF ISO 9001:2015 quality certification.

Our network of volunteers, professionals, and corporate partners works tirelessly across India — from food distribution and quality education, to protecting animals and planting trees.

What We Do

Our Six Key Projects

🍱 Food & Clothing

Project Seva

Providing nutritious food and clothing to underprivileged communities across India, so no one sleeps hungry or cold.

📚 Education

Project BachpanSala

Ensuring quality education reaches every underprivileged child. Nurturing young minds to build bright futures.

🐾 Animal Welfare

Project Jeev

Rescuing, protecting, and feeding animals in distress. Because compassion has no species boundary.

👩‍💼 Women Empowerment

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development and financial independence, helping them soar towards a brighter future.

🌱 Environment

Project Prakriti

Environmental conservation and sustainability efforts — planting trees and inspiring communities to protect the planet.

🛠️ Skill Development

Project Vikas

Enhancing employability through targeted skill development programs, opening doors for those left behind by the system.

Why It Matters

The Purpose Behind Our Work

Social Inclusion

We bridge the gap between the privileged and the marginalized, creating programs that ensure every individual has access to basic rights, dignity, and opportunity.

Community Resilience

By empowering local volunteers across 28 states, we build lasting community resilience that does not depend on external support to sustain itself.

Transparent Impact

Our ISO certifications and government registrations ensure every rupee donated is tracked, accounted for, and directed to those who need it most.

National Reach

With 200+ volunteers spanning 28 states, InAmigos is proving that collective, decentralized action can create nationwide change — one community at a time.
